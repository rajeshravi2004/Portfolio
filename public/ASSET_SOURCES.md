# Brand asset sources

Brand marks identify technologies and organizations; they do not imply endorsement.

- Existing technology SVGs: Devicon (https://devicon.dev/).
- Qdrant, LangChain, Google Gemini, Swagger, OpenAPI Initiative, Stripe, Razorpay, aiohttp, YouTube: Simple Icons (https://simpleicons.org/), downloaded through https://cdn.simpleicons.org/ on 2026-09-06. Check individual brand guidelines before repurposing.
- Mittai app mark: developer-published Google Play listing, https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mittai .
- CareScribe wordmark: https://carescribe.health/carescribe-logo.png .
- AIIRF mark: https://aiirf.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/AIIRF-logo-Copy.png .

All marks are served locally. Concept icons (for example REST APIs and RAG) are inline SVGs rather than invented company logos.
